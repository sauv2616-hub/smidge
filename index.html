      How Smidge works

      • You get four quick bakes a day. Some use your fingers, like cutting dough into equal pieces. Some use the phone itself: tip it to pour oil, shake it to sift flour, turn it like an oven dial.
      • There is no measuring cup, no scale and no clock. Go by feel.
      • Each bake scores up to 100. The closer you get, the higher it goes.
      • Everyone gets the same four, and you get one go. A new Smidge lands at midnight.
      • Your phone will ask to share motion. Tap Allow to play by tipping and shaking.
      • Smidge is much better on a phone. On a computer, or without motion access, you get mouse and touch versions instead.
      • After every bake you see your score and how you did against other bakers. The home screen keeps your streak.
