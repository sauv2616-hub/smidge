      How Smidge works

      • You get five quick gut calls a day: cut a line, count a flash of dots, stop a hidden clock, and so on.
      • No ruler, no counting on your fingers, no watch. Trust your eye.
      • Each call scores up to 100. The closer you get, the higher it goes.
      • Everyone gets the same five, and you get one go. A new Smidge lands at midnight.
