Privacy

Smidge is a small daily game. It has no accounts and asks for no personal details.

What stays on your device

Your scores, streak and sound setting are saved in your browser's local storage so the game remembers you tomorrow. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Motion sensors

Some games read your phone's tilt and movement so you can pour, shake and turn. Those readings are used on your device while you play and are never stored or sent anywhere.

What is sent when you finish

When you finish the daily game, Smidge adds one to an anonymous counter for the score band you landed in (for example "85 to 90"). That is how the daily curve is drawn. No name, account or exact score is attached. The counters are hosted by a third-party counting service (Abacus), which, like any web server, sees the IP address of each request.

Fonts

Typefaces load from Google Fonts, so Google's servers receive a standard request from your browser.

Advertising

Smidge does not show ads at the moment. If that changes, this page will be updated first to name the ad provider and explain how cookies and consent work.
